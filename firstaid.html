KNOW WHAT TO DO IN AN EMERGENCY

SECTION 04

WHAT IS DRSABCD?

DRSABCD is an Australian first-aid action plan used when someone needs emergency assistance and it is safe to approach them.

It helps first aiders remember the important steps when responding to an emergency.

IMPORTANT: Never enter dangerous water to reach someone. Alert a lifeguard and call 000 for emergency assistance. Only provide first aid when it is safe.

THE SEVEN STEPS OF DRSABCD

D

DANGER

Check that the area is safe for yourself, bystanders and the person needing help.

R

RESPONSE

Check whether the person responds. Ask their name and gently squeeze their shoulders.

S

SEND FOR HELP

Call Triple Zero (000) for an ambulance or ask someone nearby to call.

A

AIRWAY

Check the person's airway and follow your first-aid training.

B

BREATHING

Check for normal breathing. Look, listen and feel for up to 10 seconds.

C

CPR

If the person is unresponsive and not breathing normally, start CPR following your training and emergency operator instructions.

D

DEFIBRILLATION

Use an automated external defibrillator (AED) as soon as available and follow its voice instructions.

WHY IS FIRST AID IMPORTANT?

Knowing basic first aid can help people respond appropriately during emergencies while waiting for professional medical assistance.

  • It helps people recognise when emergency assistance is needed.
  • It provides a clear action plan to follow.
  • It encourages people to get professional help quickly.
  • It can help someone receive assistance while waiting for an ambulance.
First-aid information is not a replacement for practical training. Always follow emergency operator instructions and recognised first-aid guidance.

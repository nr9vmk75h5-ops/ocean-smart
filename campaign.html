HOW CAN OUR OCEAN SMART CAMPAIGN HELP?

Our Ocean Smart campaign is designed to improve beach safety by educating teenagers, families and international visitors about rip currents and other ocean hazards.

OUR CAMPAIGN USES:

A4 Safety Poster

A clear poster with important beach safety information.

Pictures and Diagrams

Visual explanations of rip currents and beach hazards.

QR Code

A QR code linking to this detailed Ocean Smart website.

Emergency Instructions

Simple instructions for preventing and responding to emergencies.

Inclusive Language

Information that people from different backgrounds can understand.

HOW WILL WE KNOW IF IT WORKS?

We can evaluate whether the campaign works by asking people if they understand the warning signs, know how to avoid rips and know what to do in an emergency.

Their feedback can help us improve the information. The poster gives people quick safety messages, while the QR guide provides more detailed information.

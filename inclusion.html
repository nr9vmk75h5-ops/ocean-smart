SECTION 06

WATER SAFETY FOR EVERYONE

Water safety information should be available to everyone, regardless of their background, culture, language, disability or swimming ability.

Some international visitors may have little experience with Australian surf beaches. Instead of judging or blaming people for not understanding the risks, we should provide clear information and support.

OUR CAMPAIGN ENCOURAGES:

Respect

Respect towards people with different swimming abilities.

Equal Access

Equal access to water safety information.

Welcoming Attitudes

Welcoming attitudes towards visitors from different cultures.

Challenging Stereotypes

Challenging assumptions about people's swimming abilities.

Community Safety

Looking after one another in the aquatic environment.

MAKING INFORMATION ACCESSIBLE

  • Use simple and clear language.
  • Include pictures and diagrams.
  • Provide translated safety information.
  • Make text easy to read.
  • Provide accessible information for people with disabilities.

Everyone deserves to feel welcome and safe at the beach. This helps build positive relationships between visitors and local communities.

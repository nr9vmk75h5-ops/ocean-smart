KNOW THE RISKS

UNDERSTANDING THE DANGERS OF RIP CURRENTS

SECTION 02

WHY ARE RIP CURRENTS DANGEROUS?

Rip currents can be dangerous because they move water away from the shore. Swimmers who enter a rip may struggle to return to the beach.

01. Being Carried Away

A rip can carry swimmers away from the shoreline, making it difficult to reach shallow water.

02. Exhaustion

Trying to swim directly against a strong current can quickly use up a person's energy.

03. Panic

People who do not understand rip currents may panic and make unsafe decisions.

04. Drowning

If a swimmer becomes exhausted or cannot keep themselves afloat, they can be at risk of drowning.

05. Changing Conditions

Rip currents can change position and conditions can vary, making the beach difficult to judge.

06. Unsafe Rescues

Someone attempting an untrained rescue may also get caught in dangerous water.

REMEMBER: Even confident swimmers can get caught in rip currents. Always swim between the red and yellow flags.

WHO CAN BE AT RISK?

  • People unfamiliar with Australian surf beaches.
  • Inexperienced swimmers.
  • People swimming outside supervised areas.
  • People who do not understand beach warning signs.
  • Swimmers who ignore lifeguard instructions.

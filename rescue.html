SECTION 05

WHAT IF YOU SEE SOMEONE ELSE IN TROUBLE?

It can be frightening to see someone struggling in the water, but it is important not to put yourself in danger.

WHAT YOU SHOULD DO

01

ALERT

Alert a lifeguard or lifesaver immediately.

02

CALL 000

Call emergency services if assistance is needed.

03

COMMUNICATE

Tell the person to stay calm and float if you can communicate safely.

04

FOLLOW ADVICE

Follow instructions from lifeguards and emergency services.

DO NOT ATTEMPT AN UNSAFE RESCUE

You could become another person needing rescue if you enter dangerous water without proper training.

WHY IS IT IMPORTANT TO GET HELP?

Lifeguards and lifesavers have specialised training and equipment to respond to aquatic emergencies. Getting professional assistance helps avoid putting additional people in danger.

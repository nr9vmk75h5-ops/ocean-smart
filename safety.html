HOW TO PREVENT GETTING CAUGHT IN A RIP

Prevention is the most important part of beach safety.

BEFORE ENTERING THE WATER

  1. Choose a beach patrolled by lifeguards or lifesavers.
  2. Always swim between the red and yellow flags.
  3. Check beach safety signs and warnings.
  4. Look for possible rip-current signs.
  5. Ask a lifeguard about conditions if unsure.
  6. Only swim if confident in your swimming ability.
  7. Swim with someone else rather than going alone.

WHILE SWIMMING

  • Stay within the supervised swimming area.
  • Pay attention to changing waves and currents.
  • Follow lifeguard instructions.
  • Leave the water if conditions become dangerous.

WHAT IF YOU GET CAUGHT IN A RIP?

01

STAY CALM

Try not to panic. Conserve your energy.

02

RAISE YOUR ARM

Call out and signal for help.

03

FLOAT

Float with the current while waiting for assistance.

04

DON'T FIGHT

Do not try to swim directly against the current.

Confident swimmers may be able to swim parallel to the beach or towards breaking waves to escape. Only attempt this if able to do so safely. If unsure, keep floating and signalling for help.

BEACH FLAGS

🔴🟡 Red and Yellow Flags

Swim between these flags. This is the supervised swimming area.

🔴 Red Flag

The beach is closed. Do not enter the water.

🟡 Yellow Flag

Conditions may be hazardous. Follow beach warnings and lifeguard instructions.
